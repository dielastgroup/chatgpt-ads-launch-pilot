## Preflight to move from preview to revenue
1. Confirm brand domain, contact inbox / working form receiver; test receiving a real submission.
2. Connect calendaring and payment or invoice checkout; submit and reconcile test checkout.
3. Confirm OpenAI Ads account and eligibility (currently none accessible via connector).
4. Add valid measurement destination (GA4 / consent implementation, platform pixel/CAPI where supported) and verify test events and lead-to-booked-to-paid reconciliation.
5. Review actual promo eligibility/conditions and approve finite spend cap before activation.
6. Publish case-study scorecard with spend, unique visitors, qualified calls, customers, fees, labor and net contribution. Do not claim profitability based on clicks alone.

## Experiment stopping rules
Pause and audit if missing lead capture, tracking failures, ineligible campaign or questionable ad delivery. Begin with small verified spend increments, compare qualified-call cost to actual closes. No claims of guaranteed ROI.
